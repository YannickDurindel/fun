# 12. Cross-platform funnel and monetisation (Instagram alpine facts/mood + X Stoic)

Researched 2026-11-16. Grades: **P** = primary (platform, regulator, official help page, or company statement fetched directly), **S** = secondary (press, blogs, vendor pages), **U** = unverified or conflicting. Dates are publication dates of the source. "Fetched" means the page was retrieved this session; "snippet" means it appeared only in a search-result summary and was not opened.

Tooling note: the shared web-search budget ran out mid-session, and about a third of the fetches returned 403 (x.com help, bookshop.org, rei.com, mediapost, Ropes & Gray). Several planned checks (Instagram bonuses status, Threads cross-post data, real post-September X payouts, AWIN/Impact programme terms, digital-product benchmarks) could not be completed. They are marked as gaps rather than filled from memory. No peer-reviewed paper on any of these topics was found in the searches run; the evidence base here is platform documents, regulators and trade press.

## 1. Headline findings

1. **X's payout programme changed in 2026 and the old numbers are obsolete.** Creator Revenue Sharing (ad share from reply threads) was retired on 7 Sep 2026 and replaced by Original Content Rewards (OCR), which pays on qualified impressions of *original* posts. X has published no per-impression rate (P/S, Aug 2026). Every "RPM" figure circulating comes from SEO blogs and is not usable for planning.
2. **OCR's originality rule is a direct risk for a Stoic quote page.** Reposted, copied or "lightly edited" content is ineligible; public-domain quotations with a card around them may not count as "net new" (our inference from X's stated rules, see s.3).
3. **Amazon Associates pays little in our niches.** Secondary sources put Outdoors/Sports at 3% and physical books around 4.5%, down from 5.5-8% in earlier years (S, 2026). Amazon needs 3 qualified sales in 180 days or the account is at risk (P).
4. **Disclosure is non-negotiable and now applies in the EU to AI content too.** FTC: affiliate links must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously, with the endorsement, not behind "more" (P). EU AI Act Article 50 deployer duties started 2 Aug 2026 (S, law-firm commentary). Instagram cuts reach of undisclosed AI *personas* (S, Aug/Sep 2026); a faceless alpine page with a human-less brand is probably not in scope, but AI-generated imagery may receive the automatic "AI info" label.
5. **Realistic revenue evidence is thin.** No independent income survey of niche faceless pages was found; all numbers are self-reported by course sellers (U).

## 3. X: Original Content Rewards (OCR) and payout evidence

**Timeline (S, consistent across TechCrunch fetched 2026-08-08, Complex, and others):**
- Early 2026: X "more than doubled" the revenue-sharing pool (no dollar figure) and offered a $1M prize for the top Article (US only, 1,000+ words), announced 16-17 Jan 2026 (S, ppc.land, fetched). Nikita Bier: "creators will only be rewarded for bangers that make it to Timeline."
- March 2026: Musk reversed some changes after backlash; April: payments to aggregator/"clickbait" accounts cut (S, TechCrunch).
- 8 Aug 2026: OCR announced; new Revenue Sharing enrolment closed; existing participants earn through 7 Sep; applications for OCR from 8 Sep (S, TechCrunch quoting X's Allegra Jacchia: the old programme's "incentives were misaligned"; "Creators should be focused on bringing net new content to the platform instead of maximizing payouts").
- Payouts biweekly; first OCR payment reported for 25 Sep 2026 (S, one source, elev8or.io, fetched; not corroborated).

**Eligibility**

| Item | Value | Conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Subscription | Active Premium / Premium+ / Premium Business | consistent |
| Verified followers | 500 | consistent |
| Impressions | 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in prior 90 days (TechCrunch, Complex, elev8or) | One blog (dev.to) says 5 million; that matches the *old* program's threshold and is likely stale. Resolve against X's own help page (403 in this session) |
| Counts | Replies, paid and artificially generated views excluded (Complex); only Premium-user impressions with at least 50% of the post visible are "qualified" (elev8or) | single-source for the 50% rule |
| Appeals | Applications via Creator Studio; one appeal; 90-day wait to reapply (Complex) | |

**Originality rules (S, Complex and TechCrunch):** eligible are original reporting, analysis, firsthand accounts, commentary, and memes, photos, videos or illustrations the poster created. Ineligible: copied posts, re-uploaded downloads, "lightly edited" content, repeated like/repost/follow bait, posts teaching others to maximise payouts, and posts that receive a helpful Community Note.

**Payout evidence.** None of the fetched articles gives a per-impression rate or any creator payout figure for OCR (TechCrunch, Complex, dev.to, elev8or all say none is published). Pre-OCR figures from a WebSearch summary ranged from about $8-12 per million impressions to $0.5-5 per thousand, a roughly 50x spread, sourced to SEO and calculator sites (U). A Nuelink article (fetched) states that "Twitter never published a clear RPM or payout formula" and mentions individual screenshots of "$50 to $263K" (the latter attributed to MrBeast), which is an outlier not a benchmark (S/U). **Do not plan around any RPM number.** The realistic framing: a small account must first clear 500 verified followers and 500k verified-Home-Timeline impressions in 90 days; earnings beyond that are unknown.

**Implication for Stoic content (our inference, graded U):** posts that are only a Marcus Aurelius/Seneca/Epictetus quote over a template resemble "lightly edited" or recycled material. Original angles (a modern worked example, a translation we make ourselves, a short essay or Article, an original graphic) fit the stated "net new value" test better. Whether X's classifier treats public-domain quotes as ineligible is not documented.

## 4. Instagram monetisation availability

- **Subscriptions** (S, Creatorflow guide fetched, updated Nov 2026): Professional account, 10,000+ followers, 18+, in one of 49 eligible countries (US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Japan, India, Brazil, Mexico named). Access is not guaranteed even if eligible. Instagram's own share is currently 0% with Apple/Google taking about 30%; Meta's Subscription Creator Terms effective 15 Oct 2025 let it add a share later. Payout minimums: $5 US, $25 elsewhere. The page was not confirmed against Instagram's help centre (an official help-centre snippet was seen but truncated), so grade S, and country list may change.
- **Bonuses:** I could not confirm any active Instagram Reels bonus programme in 2026 (search budget exhausted before checking). The Creatorflow page does not describe one. Treat bonuses as **unavailable/unknown (U)** and do not budget for them. Our channel is a faceless page; a profile with no real person is fine for subscriptions but the 10k floor means it is a month-6+ question at best.
- **AI labelling and reach (S, snippets of MediaPost/TechRepublic/others, MediaPost and TechRepublic fetches returned 403; date reported 31 Aug or 2 Sep 2026):** Instagram renamed "AI creator" to "AI-generated profile" and limits the reach of profiles that present an AI-generated person without the label. Using AI only for editing or captions does not trigger it. The post-level "AI info" label is added automatically when Instagram detects AI tools. Implication: label AI-generated imagery of landscapes honestly; do not present synthetic people.

| Evidence | Grade | Note |

No peer-reviewed or platform-published benchmark for Reel-to-carousel or Instagram-to-X funnels was found. Design principles below are therefore engineering judgement, not evidence-based.

- Instagram: Reels discover (Q3 data show recommendation favours fresh content, S); carousels deepen (saves/shares); the bio link is the single conversion point.
- X: the funnel direction is weak. X's strongest 2026 reward is Home Timeline impressions from verified accounts, so linking out costs reach with no stated penalty or benefit (U: no fetched X documentation on link downranking). Keep links in replies or the profile.
- Email list: the only channel we own. Both platforms' algorithms changed materially in 2026 (OCR, Instagram aggregator removal), supporting the argument for owned reach, but no conversion benchmark could be verified.

| AWIN / Impact networks | not researched | not researched | Gap |

**Amazon policy facts (P, Amazon help pages fetched):**
- Statement "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." must be clearly and conspicuously on the Site; for social media user-generated content the statement "must be associated with your account", and per-link disclosures such as "(paid link)", "#ad" or "#CommissionsEarned" are suggested, near the link.
- At least three qualified sales within the first 180 days; own orders don't count; a rejected application can't be reassessed. The page does not state the consequence of missing the three-sale mark. Third-party sources say the account is closed (S).
- Listing every site or social channel where links are used in Associates Central is advised by secondary guides (S, snippet; not confirmed on a fetched Amazon page).
- **Anonymity:** I found no Amazon document either requiring or forbidding a real name on the profile. Operator identity is collected at signup (not verified here, U). The bigger issue is that Amazon links cannot be clickable in Instagram captions, so the only link surface is the bio.

**Practical read:** at 3-5% on low-ticket outdoor gear and books, affiliate income for a small page is minor. Better value is likely in higher-AOV merchants (Backcountry 8% average, if true) or in selling own digital products, but that is not evidenced here.

## 9. Realistic timelines and revenue ranges

- **Evidence quality: low.** The only data found were creator-reported case studies and course sales pages, e.g. "3-9 months" to first revenue for an AI avatar page and claims of 500K+ in 10 months (U, search snippets, promotional). A claim that niche and monetisation stack matter more than reach (a 120k AI-tools page outearning a 500k motivation page 6-10x) is a single unsourced assertion (U).
- **Hard thresholds we do know (P/S):** X OCR needs 500 verified followers + 500k verified impressions/90 days + Premium; Instagram Subscriptions need 10k followers + eligible country; Amazon needs 3 sales in 180 days.
- **Conservative planning range (our estimate, U):** months 0-6: no meaningful revenue; affiliate pennies. Months 6-12: possible OCR eligibility on X if verified-impression volume is reached; low hundreds of EUR/USD per month is the plausible ceiling with no published rate to support more. We have no data to support any figure higher than this.

## 10. Implications for us

**Do**
1. Treat X OCR eligibility as a milestone, not a plan: get Premium, accumulate 500 verified followers, and track "verified Home Timeline impressions" in X analytics monthly. Do not forecast revenue until the first payout gives a real rate.
2. Make Stoic posts original enough for the "net new value" test: our own paraphrase/translation, a modern example, an Article. Avoid bare quote-card reposting and engagement bait ("like if you agree").
3. Build an email list from day one (free alpine checklist or Stoic weekly) with a single bio link; measure our own baseline, since no reliable benchmark exists.
4. Put a per-post disclosure inside the caption's first line or on-screen ("paid link"/"#ad"), plus Amazon's statement in the profile. Disclose in the video itself for Reels with affiliate links.
5. Apply to Amazon only once the bio link has traffic (3 sales in 180 days); also consider Bookshop.org (confirmed 48-hour attribution) and Backcountry/REI via their networks after confirming rates in the network dashboards.
6. Label AI-generated imagery per Instagram's "AI info" and EU Art. 50 where realistic depictions of real places/events are synthetic.
7. Re-verify gaps below before committing spend.

**Don't**
- Don't cite any X "RPM" figure internally.
- Don't rely on Instagram bonuses (unconfirmed).
- Don't build the funnel on Threads cross-posting claims (no data).
- Don't use a fake human persona on an Instagram profile; it triggers the AI-generated-profile label and a reach cut.
- Don't assume anonymity is allowed with Amazon or networks; check signup terms (identity/tax details are normally required).
